# Office Cat sample

`sample_sheet.png` is a byte-for-byte copy of the privately owned Office Cat walk sheet:

- source: `Assets/Resources/heroes/cat/cat_office/cat.png`
- format: indexed transparent PNG, 2560×2560, 25 frames in a 5×5 grid
- PNG SHA-256: `84310781e847829d28c408145366fbe42d2065d683980451fa72d6cd08be4acf`
- metadata SHA-256: `2b64d12f4da6ebbd7842ef0a2ba0dcddc5fa27d9bbd4c67a938d0d709bc92869`

The companion `.png.meta` preserves the source Unity metadata with trailing YAML whitespace
normalized. Its tight frame rectangles let the cleaner propagate reviewed marks across the
animation. The source sheet intentionally remains dirty. The clearest defect is the opaque
warm-gray source matte enclosed by the curled tail; the pale face, eyes, shirt, cuffs, paws, and
highlights are artwork.

On 2026-08-30, the asset owner explicitly confirmed the right to publicly redistribute
the complete Office Cat PNG and metadata in this repository as a real-world test and
documentation fixture. It is not offered as reusable stock art. The Apache-2.0 licence in the
repository covers the Sprite Pocket Cleaner software; do not infer a separate art licence from
the fixture's inclusion. Downstream reuse or republication of the artwork requires its own
permission or legal basis.
