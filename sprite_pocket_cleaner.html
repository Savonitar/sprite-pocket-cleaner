Sprite Pocket Cleaner

Drop a sheet .png here (and its .png.meta, so frames and propagation work). Click a leftover background pocket — white, gray, or another matte colour — to mark it. Shift-drag marks a rectangle instead, for a fringe a flood fill will not hold.
